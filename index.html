Prime Clips Tool

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 16, 2026

Prime Clips Tool is a single-user personal tool used to publish short video clips to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. This policy explains, plainly, what the tool does and does not do with data.

01 What we store

TikTok OAuth tokens

When the operator connects their TikTok account, the tool stores the resulting OAuth access token and refresh token locally on the operator's own device. These tokens are used solely to authenticate API requests that publish video content to that same TikTok account.

No other personal data is collected. The tool does not have a server, a database, or an account system — it runs locally and talks directly to TikTok's API.

02 What we don't do

  • We do not collect data about anyone other than the operator.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not access followers, direct messages, or any account data beyond what's required to publish a video.

03 Revoking access

The operator can revoke Prime Clips Tool's access at any time from TikTok's account settings under Manage account → Apps and websites, or by deleting the locally stored tokens. Revoking access immediately stops the tool from being able to publish on the operator's behalf.

04 Retention

OAuth tokens are retained locally only for as long as the operator continues using the tool. Deleting the tool's local configuration removes them completely.

05 Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app operator via the contact details on file with TikTok for Developers.
